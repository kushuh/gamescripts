# Tales of Vesperia progress checkers

Check your progress in **Tales of Vesperia: Definitive Edition** from a Windows terminal window.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11.
- Steam and Tales of Vesperia: Definitive Edition installed.
- At least one game save stored on the computer.

## Which file to run

| Double-click | What you get |
|---|---|
| `Check-Fatal-Strikes.bat` | Each character's Fatal Strike count, strikes remaining to reach 100, and title status. |
| `Check-Save-Points.bat` | An ordered list of save-point locations, marked **Used** or **Not used yet**, grouped by story section. |
| `Check-Secret-Missions.bat` | All 25 Secret Missions and their bosses, marked **Done** or **Not done**. |
| `Check-Giganto-Monsters.bat` | All 11 Giganto monsters and their locations, marked **Defeated** or **Not defeated**, with your completion total. |

## How to use

1. Keep the `.bat` files and their matching `.ps1` files together in the same folder.
2. Save your progress in the game and wait for saving to finish.
3. Double-click the checker you want to use.
4. Read the table, scrolling up as needed. Press a key to close the window.

The checkers automatically find your most recent save. Its filename and date appear above the results; check these if you use several accounts or playthroughs.

Green statuses indicate completion; yellow statuses indicate remaining progress. In the save-point tables, `!` marks a temporary or time-sensitive location, and `*` marks entries that share the same completion status.

Rerun a checker after saving again to see your updated progress.
